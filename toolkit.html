💡 Learning Tip

Don't just ask AI for an answer. Ask it to explain, question, compare, and teach you so that you actually understand the topic.

🧠 Explain Like I'm a Beginner

Explain [TOPIC] in very simple words. Give me an everyday example, then explain the topic in more detail.

📚 Study Coach

Act as my study coach. Help me learn [SUBJECT] step by step. Ask me questions, test my understanding, and explain anything I get wrong.

🌱 Healthy Lifestyle

Help me create simple and realistic habits for a healthier lifestyle. Focus on sleep, movement, nutrition, hydration, stress management, and spending time in nature.

🌿 Connect With Nature

Give me 10 simple ways to spend more time in nature and reduce unnecessary screen time. Make the ideas practical and easy to include in my daily routine.

🎯 Goal Planner

My goal is [GOAL]. Break it into small daily and weekly steps. Help me create a realistic plan and identify what might stop me from achieving it.

💭 Think Deeper

Don't give me the answer immediately. Ask me questions that help me think critically about [TOPIC]. Then guide me toward the answer.

✨ Idea Generator

Give me 10 creative ideas for [PROJECT]. Make them useful, realistic, original, and suitable for my interests and skills.

🔍 Research Helper

Help me research [TOPIC]. Explain the important facts in simple language and clearly separate facts, opinions, assumptions, and uncertain information.

🚀 Improve My Life

Analyze my current routine: [DESCRIBE YOUR ROUTINE]. Tell me what I'm doing well, what I could improve, and give me 3 small changes I can start today.
